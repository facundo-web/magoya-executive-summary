Problem, Solution & Value

PROBLEM STATEMENT
Two platforms, one fragmented grower journey

Growers used FieldView for activities such as visualizing field data through maps or creating prescriptions, and Experto, for personalized agronomic recommendations, environment analysis and crop monitoring.

Because these capabilities lived separately, growers had to move between platforms to complete parts of the same journey, creating friction and wasting time.

SOLUTION PROVIDED
Bring Experto into FieldView

Magoya designed and delivered an integrated experience that brought Experto’s capabilities and key workflows into FieldView, while preserving FieldView’s  product sense and identity.

The solution also enabled Growers to migrate all their pre-existing data, (e.g fields) seamlessly from one platform to the other .

VALUE CREATED
An all-in-one digital experience

For Growers, the solution provides a more robust, centralized experience, with all capabilities accessible from one place.

For Bayer, the integration strengthens FieldView as the core digital customer platform, and establishes a foundation for future initiatives, integrations and commercial impact.
