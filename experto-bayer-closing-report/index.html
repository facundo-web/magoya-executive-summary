Problem, Solution & Value

Problem

Experto treated every user identically — a grower and a sales rep had the same functionalities and information.

No differentiated value for the commercial team, and no access to Bayer's internal, commercial-specific data to drive sales and revenue goals.

Solution

Make Experto the place where the commercial team works and shares growers' agronomic information for sales purposes.

One connected system: grower context, agronomic decisions and recommendations — history, benchmarks, trials — in a single continuous flow.

Experto rebuilt as a workspace where the team plans, tracks and logs field activity, with full traceability and share-ready outputs.

Value created

Delivered through deep discovery into what each role actually needs day to day: a dedicated profile per role, each surfacing the specific grower, agronomic, and commercial information that role works with — with cross-role linking so every role sees the exact same field-level agronomic data for their shared growers, instead of working from disconnected views. Consolidating Experto Bayer as the commercial team's tool.

Scope & Deliverables

Watch demo

Experto Bayer is a commercial enablement tool for sales representatives, agronomic consultants and agronomic advisors — delivering tailored recommendations, performance insights and market intelligence. It strengthens their ability to customize solutions per field, backed by agronomic data and trials. By connecting growers, positioning and product recommendations, it delivers strategic guidance that creates value for both the grower and the business.

THREE PILLARS

Experto is embedded in Bayer's commercial strategy, supporting the commercial team's work and grower relationships from pre-season through harvest analysis.

Jointly defined needs

Solutions aligned to Bayer's commercial and agronomic goals.

Built with the team

Understanding their needs to drive day-to-day adoption.

Built on Bayer's agronomic science

Draws on Bayer's internal agronomic data to power validated models such as corn phenological tracking and harvest drying moisture %.

SECTION 02 · DELIVERABLES

Core features & value propositions

Seven capabilities shipped in this phase, each answering a specific job of the commercial team.

01

Role-based profiles
RV · Consultant · Advisor

A dedicated experience per role, replacing the one-size-fits-all view.

Each role sees and acts only on what matters to their job — the first product truly built for them.

02

Advisor ↔ RV linking

Shared access to grower and field data across roles.

Coordinated grower coverage instead of duplicated, disconnected outreach.

03

Squad Coach dashboard

Cross-team visibility into RV, Consultant and Advisor activity by squad.

Gives commercial leadership a real-time read on team performance — a first for Bayer.

04

Campaign management data

Historical campaign timeline per field.

Grounds every recommendation in what actually happened on that field.

05

Product Explorer

The full Bayer product catalogue with characteristics, competitive advantages and head-to-head comparisons.

Puts commercial evidence for every product in the rep's hands during the sales conversation.

06

Campaign Report

The grower's campaign data, including which Bayer products they used.

Shows where Bayer already is on the farm — and where the opportunity still sits.

07

Visit report

Structured record of each grower visit, logged directly by the commercial team.

A documented, shareable paper trail of every grower interaction.

Project Highlights

Field trial & product data at the core

Experto's backend aggregates Argentina's field trial network data and Bayer's commercial value proposition per product — keeping corn hybrid and crop protection recommendations updated with fresh data every year, now feeding both Experto and FieldView.

Seamless FieldView integration

Two-way sync of planting, application and harvest data between Experto and Climate FieldView — eliminating double data entry and keeping recommendations grounded in real field conditions.

Marketing & communication for feature rollouts

Every major feature launch was paired with dedicated marketing and communication — targeted email campaigns and role-specific rollout messaging — to drive adoption from day one.

User enablement through training

Rollout was backed by hands-on enablement for the commercial team: digital content, online meetings and in-person training sessions.

Purpose-built reporting for stakeholders

A dedicated reporting product built specifically for Bayer's key stakeholders — full visibility into the main usage and business metrics, across both Argentina and Uruguay.
